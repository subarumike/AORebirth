  interpolation position.

  arithmetic.

  attack/recharge cadence, attack rating, defense, and other derived stats.

  mutable SAW state.
- Capture contracts own mode, slot, instance semantics, packet structures,
  N3/action fields, numeric hit/damage types, genuine stream count/order, and

Source identity, captured generation identity, mutable state, observed damage,
observed timing/range, and production-selected QL are not reusable archetype
identity. Source rows still select their own authoritative active generation;
they are not formula branches or cross-enemy mappings.
