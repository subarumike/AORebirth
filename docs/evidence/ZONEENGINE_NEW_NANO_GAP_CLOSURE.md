## Reopened area and precise repair

`EXISTING_AREA_REOPENED=Nano specialization completion and active inventory planning`

`WHY_REQUIRED=Sparrow82835 was rejected because its exact nested CastNano273292 was missing`

`BLOCKER_THAT_PROVED_IT=Tracked nanos.dat parent graph plus compiled Legacy castnano.ApplyInstantNano and existing morph capture wires`

N01 adds one narrowly admitted scripted-child plan, not a recursive general interpreter. Parent and admitted child active rows, exact NCU and parent mana cost are committed once before success/effect publication. Child casting has no extra upload requirement, mana charge, attack delay or recharge. Its own1cs duration and999NCU come from the actual catalog. Missing/changed child graph fails preflight. Parent/child instances are distinct; refresh preserves existing identity. Existing snapshot and unknown-commit protections remain authoritative.

Legacy's nested child function executes **OnUse only**. Child273292 contains seven OnTerminate RemoveNanoStrain entries but no OnUse; existing active-nano expiry/removal does not dispatch OnTerminate. These removals and the parent's own termination cast are therefore not invented in New. A future termination engine is not a prerequisite for supported-source Sparrow parity.

The first focused run exposed a child-specific descriptor mistake: the real child has one Defend entry, while the parent has none. Treating that descriptor as an executable hostile effect rejected Sparrow even though the exact self-scripted child has no OnUse and Legacy does not perform resistance resolution there. The narrow child specialization now accepts its declared descriptor without inventing a resistance roll; the generic hostile-effect guard is unchanged. The offline catalog artifact now includes Attack/Defend descriptors for this audit.

`LEGACY_BUG_NOT_PORTED=SetNanoDuration sent when child NCU admission failed and no active row existed`

New still emits the accepted nested CastNano/Finish after the parent commit, but does not advertise a nonexistent duration. If999NCU fits alongside the parent, the real child row joins that same transaction and its exact duration is sent. This preserves server/client active-state ownership instead of recreating the ghost row. Parent flight remains governed by current non-SL playfield authority on zone refresh, including an initial SL cast followed by RK entry.

## Team Warp and reopened transfer scheduling

`EXISTING_AREA_REOPENED=Existing ZoneSession/Playfield cross-playfield transfer scheduling`

`WHY_REQUIRED=Team Warp invokes transfers from recipient owner ticks; the prior synchronous destination lock permits reciprocal source/destination AB/BA deadlock`

`BLOCKER_THAT_PROVED_IT=ZoneSession.TransferToPlayfield synchronously called destination.ArriveTransferredPlayer while the source tick remained held`

The repair retains the existing Player, registry, hard-flush, teleport and redirection authorities. Departure is queued to the source; arrival is queued to the destination; a rejected/disposed destination queues return to the source. Per-session finite phases and both playfields' pending sets make shutdown disposition explicit. Source shutdown restores a departed owner to its original position before the ordinary snapshot/logout pass. Destination collision never replaces another dynel; a replaced global owner cannot receive an old snapshot. No transition acquires the other playfield's tick lock. The hard flush takes PersistenceGate before the session lock, matching write-behind failure/Close ordering. A transport close cannot bind an old session over a replacement. Successful redirection enters the existing LinkDead reconnect grace without detaching team membership: a reconnect clears that deadline, while an abandoned redirect expires through the ordinary destination snapshot/logout and releases the lease exactly once. No new timeout or protocol was invented.

Warp154914 resolves an explicit nonzero request target at completion, otherwise the then-current selection. Warp154913 uses the exact team roster order, excludes self, skips missing online owners, and consumes a landing slot for each resolved online member even when its playfield is restricted. Landing retains the accepted two-meter rotated offset with slot angle `slot * 0.7f`, current caster position and heading. The New motor's current transform is its existing movement authority; no second prediction engine is added. Both warp nanos retain catalog requirements, attack, recharge and caster cost, but create no NCU row and impose no invented one-meter/LOS limit on the dedicated cross-RK path.

Preparation reads caster stats on the caster tick and only exact member/session/PF references across playfields. A nano-owned interruption generation fences deferred effects without reading a foreign caster's Stats dictionary. Member health and movement are checked/mutated on its owner. Team, caster and member authority are rechecked before queued movement and again at cross-PF departure/arrival; failed authorization returns a departed member. Same-PF wire order remains Despawn, SetPos, visibility/SCFU, Appearance and self SCFU, limited to the existing visibility-recipient filter with exact sessions. Postcommit callbacks cannot be replayed. Invalid preflight does not charge cost; an interruption after a successful cost commit can safely cancel pending movement, not refund/replay the durable cast.

`PlayfieldTransferTests` covers source flush ordering, destination queuing, deterministic reciprocal transfers, writer-close lock order, flush failure, destination rejection/disposal, transport close/rebind, shutdown order and replacement actor fences. `TeamWarpNanoTests` covers actual154914/154913 catalog timing/cost/no-NCU, packet ordering, current-selection semantics, roster offsets, restrictions, rollback/quarantine, session/team/zone fences and post-departure caster cancellation. Fixtures are source-contract reproductions, not relabeled live captures.
