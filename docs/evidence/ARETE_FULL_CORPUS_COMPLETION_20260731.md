# Arete authoritative acceptance matrix — 2026-07-31

## Acceptance decision

**Arete is complete for the behavior supported by the complete existing evidence corpus.** This file is the authoritative Arete acceptance matrix at and after commit `a83d689a`; older generated plans, phase notes, live-verification notes, TODOs, and blocker statements are historical when they disagree with this matrix.

A behavior is accepted only when the corpus proves the implemented boundary, production owns that boundary, and a focused test guards the owner. Facts that the corpus does not prove are listed separately as non-blocking evidence gaps. They do not downgrade or block supported behavior, and they are not filled with guessed defaults.

Evidence strength is stated explicitly:

- **Exact**: the corpus proves the value, ordering, identity, packet sequence, timing, or geometry used by production.
- **Range**: the corpus proves a bounded measured range, not a single universal value.
- **Eligibility/direction**: the corpus proves that a behavior occurs or which direction it takes, but not its probability or hidden activation threshold.
- **Unresolved probability**: captured outcomes exist, but their selection distribution is not observable.
- **Absent**: the searched corpus contains no evidence for the missing fact.

## Acceptance suite

The authoritative release gate is `tools\run_arete_acceptance_tests.cmd`, followed by `tools\build_aorebirth_debug.cmd` and `restart-engines.cmd`. ## Evidence-discipline confirmation

No available evidence was ignored because of an invented rule. Cross-capture identity/lifecycle correlation was used where it proved the relationship; independently classified clean observations remained usable even when another observation in the route group was rejected; combat/player influence was retained in chase, flee, and leash rather than discarded; and measured values were preferred over defaults. No new capture, client login, closed-loop requirement, prior-state gate, single-packet-subtype requirement, or repeated observation was required for a behavior already proved by the repository corpus.
