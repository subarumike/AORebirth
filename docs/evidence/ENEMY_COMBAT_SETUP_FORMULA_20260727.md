# Enemy Combat Setup Formula

Date: 2026-07-27

## Result

The twelve active Disobedient Bots formerly quarantined at L6/L7/L9/L10 now
receive deterministic numeric combat setup from their existing runtime level.
No user-supplied level, actor identity, per-level production table,
nearest-level selection, neighboring-value copy, packet replay, or fallback
combat is used.

The exact accepted formula is:

`SAW.Unknown1..4 = floor((19 * actorLevel + 28) / 4)`

It is implemented as positive checked integer arithmetic, so C# truncation is
exactly floor. There is no clamp inside the formula. The generator instead
fails closed unless all domain predicates match: MonsterData `17649`, actor
level `5..10`, low/high templates `144742/144743`, tag `0x53495731`, and name
`SIW1`.

## Formula Families Tested

Programmatic exact rational search considered affine integer/rational
expressions with floor, ceiling, nearest-away, and nearest-even rounding,
integer division, intercepts, denominators through 128, and the required
bounded-domain branch. It also evaluated direct `items.dat` endpoint
interpolation and reuse across other families sharing templates
`144742/144743`.

The selected representation is the simplest exact floor form. At denominator
4, the only three matching representations are mathematically equivalent on
integer levels:

- `floor((19L + 28) / 4)`;
- `ceil((19L + 25) / 4)`;
- nearest-away on `(19L + 26) / 4`.

Every wider exact-search candidate predicts L7=`40`.

Rejected candidates:

- Exact unrounded linear: adjacent captured slopes differ, so it cannot
  reproduce all five points.
- Direct item-template QL interpolation: the QL1/QL400 stats for
  `144742/144743` do not produce `30,35,45,49,54` from actor level.
- One SIW1 formula across enemy families: only 7 of 27 other-family
  observations match; MonsterData/family is therefore an exact selector.
- Unbounded extrapolation: no categorical proof exists below L5 or above L10.

## Leave-One-Out and Cross-Family Validation

For each of L5/L6/L8/L9/L10, the point was removed, the accepted exact formula
was evaluated against the remaining four observations, and the held-out value
was predicted. Results were `30`, `35`, `45`, `49`, and `54`, respectively:
five of five exact, with zero field or byte differences.

Cross-family validation rejects generic SIW1 reuse: 20 of 27 compatible-template
observations differ. Accordingly, the production domain is the Disobedient Bot
MonsterData family, not the weapon template alone.
