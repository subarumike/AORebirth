## What the baseline contains

- 11,250 unique mission identities and 9,412 unique full descriptions.
- 3,154 distinct `(low_id, high_id)` item pairs.
- 6,205 distinct `(low_id, high_id, observed item QL, unknown)` descriptors.
- 6,798 of the 7,428 per-QL descriptor rows resolve to an All-CDB item name;
  630 remain unnamed while retaining their raw identities.
- Destinations in Clondyke, Galway County, Galway Shire, Lush Fields, and
  Mutant Domain, with the exact QL-specific counts preserved in the CSV.
- Exact raw evidence and parsed provenance remain in the source session; the
  compact CSVs do not replace that journal.

## Important coverage limitation

Only three mission types occurred in this centered-slider baseline:

| Mission type | Offers |
| --- | ---: |
| Repair | 6,750 |
| Kill Person | 2,250 |
| Return Item | 2,250 |

Find Item and Find Person were not observed in 11,250 offers. This does not
prove that they are unavailable at these QLs. It means the centered-slider
corpus is not sufficient by itself to claim complete mission-type coverage, and
it cannot establish item references specific to those absent types.

## Safe uses

The baseline is useful for observed item candidates by roller level and actual
mission QL, observed item QLs, mission-type/text/credit/XP/destination analysis,
cross-level comparisons, and packet/parser replay.

It does not prove complete item-pool membership, exclusions, probability
weights, item interpolation, separately typed objective-item identity, mission
interiors, enemies, or semantic-slider effects.

Generated by `Tools/analyze_mission_spectrum_capture.cmd` and
`Tools/analyze_mission_spectrum_capture.py`. Input paths and SHA-256 hashes are
recorded in `summary.json`.
