This baseline contains only the completed `MISSION_QL_SPECTRUM_V2` evidence for
character identity `37165191`. It uses the 2,000 durable completion records from
two sessions. Older reward-saturation and slider-discovery sessions are excluded
so they do not distort the comparable 250-request-per-QL corpus.

## What the baseline contains

- 10,000 unique mission identities and 8,211 unique full descriptions.
- 2,704 distinct `(low_id, high_id)` item pairs.
- 5,269 distinct `(low_id, high_id, observed item QL, unknown)` descriptors.
- 5,768 of the 6,259 per-QL descriptor rows resolve to an All-CDB item name;
  491 remain unnamed while retaining their raw identities.
- Destinations in Galway Shire and Lush Fields for QL 4-9, and Clondyke and
  Lush Fields for QL 10 and 12.
- Exact raw evidence and parsed provenance remain in the two source sessions;
  the compact CSVs do not replace those journals.

## Important coverage limitation

Only three mission types occurred in this centered-slider baseline:

| Mission type | Offers |
| --- | ---: |
| Repair | 6,000 |
| Kill Person | 2,000 |
| Return Item | 2,000 |

Find Item and Find Person were not observed in 10,000 offers. This does not
prove that they are unavailable at these QLs. It means the centered-slider
corpus is not sufficient by itself to claim complete mission-type coverage, and
it cannot establish item references specific to those absent types.

## Safe uses

The baseline is useful for observed item candidates by roller level and actual
mission QL, observed item QLs, mission-type/text/credit/XP/destination analysis,
cross-level comparisons, and packet/parser replay.

It does not prove complete item-pool membership, exclusions, probability
weights, item interpolation, separately typed objective-item identity, mission
interiors, enemies, or semantic-slider effects.

Generated by `Tools/analyze_mission_spectrum_capture.cmd` and
`Tools/analyze_mission_spectrum_capture.py`. Input paths and SHA-256 hashes are
recorded in `summary.json`.
